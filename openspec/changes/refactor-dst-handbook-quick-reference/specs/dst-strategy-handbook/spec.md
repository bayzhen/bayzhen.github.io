## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Quick lookup handbook structure
The handbook SHALL organize strategy content for fast reference, prioritizing day/season state lookup before broader topic essays, and using consistent headings, tables, checklists, and cross-links rather than long unstructured essays.

#### Scenario: Reader needs an immediate answer by current day
- **WHEN** a reader opens the handbook while playing on a known day range such as day 1-3, day 8-15, day 21 winter, or day 56 summer
- **THEN** the handbook exposes a day/season quick reference that tells the reader what to do, what to avoid, and what to prepare next

#### Scenario: Reader needs an immediate answer by system
- **WHEN** a reader needs details for cooking, farming, or character-specific systems
- **THEN** the handbook links to focused system reference pages rather than forcing the reader through broad survival essays

#### Scenario: Reader scans repeated article patterns
- **WHEN** a reader moves between handbook articles
- **THEN** recurring sections such as "结论速查", "优先级", "常见错误", and "相关条目" use consistent names and placement

### Requirement: Coverage of practical DST strategy domains
The handbook SHALL cover the core survival and progression domains needed for practical Don't Starve Together play, from early survival through long-term world management, and SHALL include dedicated quick-reference coverage for seasonal planning, cooking, farming, and character/special systems.

#### Scenario: New player follows the series
- **WHEN** a newer player reads the opening chapters in order
- **THEN** the content first explains what to do by day/season, then expands into first-week survival, base placement, food, sanity, health, tools, light, and seasonal preparation

#### Scenario: Experienced player jumps to a topic
- **WHEN** an experienced player uses the handbook as a reference
- **THEN** the series includes focused entries for character roles, combat preparation, Boss planning, caves/ruins, cooking, farming, crisis recovery, and late-game loops

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Day and season decision table
The handbook SHALL include a dedicated day/season cheatsheet that maps common default-world day ranges and season states to actions, avoidances, and next preparations.

#### Scenario: Reader checks winter day range
- **WHEN** a reader is in early winter
- **THEN** the cheatsheet tells them to stabilize warmth, food, light, armor, and Boss readiness, and warns against long unprepared trips

#### Scenario: Reader checks summer day range
- **WHEN** a reader is in early summer
- **THEN** the cheatsheet tells them to manage overheating, wildfire, cooling, and surface-vs-cave activity decisions

### Requirement: System reference pages
The handbook SHALL include dedicated system reference pages for cooking, farming, and character/special systems with practical usage rules and links back to the day/season plan.

#### Scenario: Reader needs cooking details
- **WHEN** a reader wants to know what to cook now
- **THEN** the cooking page provides beginner-safe recipes, ingredient rules, and which situations each food solves

#### Scenario: Reader needs farming details
- **WHEN** a reader wants to know whether and how to farm
- **THEN** the farming page explains farm timing, stress sources, nutrients, moisture, weeds, and practical low-maintenance patterns

#### Scenario: Reader needs character details
- **WHEN** a reader chooses or changes a character
- **THEN** the character page explains role selection, team fit, skill trees/Insight, and where to verify current character-specific mechanics
