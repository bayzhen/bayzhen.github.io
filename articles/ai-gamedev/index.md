## About This Series

What if you could describe a game and an AI builds it for you? This series follows that dream from initial excitement through technical reality to a surprising conclusion. We start with the promise of AI game development, examine real tools like MCP that try to bridge AI and game engines, watch them struggle against Unreal Engine's complexity, and arrive at a deeper question: what actually limits AI in game development?

## What You'll Find

- **Article 1 — The Dream** What if AI could build games for us?
- **Article 2 — MCP: A Promising Answer** The protocol that lets AI talk to tools, and the teams already using it
- **Article 3 — How AI Talks to an Engine** Architecture deep dive into connecting AI to Unreal Engine
- **Article 4 — Where It Breaks** Latency, token waste, and the reflection trap
- **Article 5 — The Wrong Target** Plot twist: maybe the problem isn't MCP — it's the engine
- **Article 6 — The Real Ceiling** The deepest insight: AI's limit is our own understanding of game design
- **Article 7 — A Practical Path Forward** Three concrete techniques that bridge AI and game engines today

## How to Use This Series

Each article is 300-500 words — short enough to copy out by hand in one sitting. Difficult words are annotated inline. The series tells a story: read in order for the full arc.

Start with [The Dream](01-the-dream.html).
