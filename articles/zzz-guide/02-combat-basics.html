Combat System Basics (战斗系统基础)

Beginner | 8-10 min read

Combat Overview (战斗概述)

ZZZ features real-time action combat (实时动作战斗) where you control a team of up to 3 agents. Combat is fast-paced (快节奏的) and emphasizes (强调) timing, positioning (走位), and team coordination (团队配合).

Basic Controls (基础操作)

Attack Types (攻击类型)

Action (动作) Input Description (描述)
Basic Attack (普通攻击) LMB / Square Standard combo (标准连招), varies by agent
Special Attack (特殊攻击) RMB / Triangle Unique skill (独特技能), often has cooldown (冷却时间)
Dodge (闪避) Space / Circle Evade (躲避) enemy attacks
Switch Agent (切换角色) 1/2/3 / L1/R1 Swap (交换) to another team member
Ultimate (终结技) Q / L2+R2 Powerful finisher (强力终结技) when energy full

The Daze System (失衡系统)

Daze (失衡值) is one of the most important mechanics (机制) in ZZZ. Every enemy has a Daze meter (失衡条) that fills up as you attack them.

Daze Buildup (失衡积累): When you hit enemies, you accumulate (积累) Daze based on your agent's Impact (冲击力) stat and skill multipliers (技能倍率).
Stun State (眩晕状态): When Daze reaches 100%, the enemy becomes stunned (眩晕) and takes increased damage (增加伤害).

Stun Duration by Enemy Type (不同敌人的眩晕时长)

Enemy Type (敌人类型) Stun Duration (眩晕时长) DMG Multiplier (伤害倍率)
Normal (普通) 2 seconds 125%
Elite (精英) 8-10 seconds 150%
Boss (首领) 10-13 seconds 150-200%
Pro Tip (专业技巧):
Stun agents (眩晕型角色) like Koleda and Lycaon have high Impact and can fill the Daze meter quickly. Use them to stun enemies, then switch to Attack agents (攻击型角色) for maximum damage!

Chain Attacks (连携攻击)

When an enemy is stunned, you can trigger Chain Attacks (连携攻击) by using a heavy attack (重击). This initiates (发动) a powerful coordinated (协调的) assault.

Chain Attack Limits (连携攻击次数限制)

  • Normal enemies (普通敌人): 1 chain attack
  • Elite enemies (精英敌人): 2 chain attacks
  • Boss enemies (首领敌人): 3 chain attacks
Bangboo Chain Attack (邦布连携攻击):
If you have a Bangboo in your party, it can also perform (执行) a special chain attack with its own cooldown (独立冷却). This doesn't count toward (不计入) the regular chain attack limit!

Dodge Mechanics (闪避机制)

Perfect Dodge (完美闪避)

Dodging at the exact moment (精确时刻) an enemy attack lands triggers a Perfect Dodge (完美闪避). This has several benefits (好处):

  • Time slows down briefly (时间短暂减缓)
  • Enables (启用) a powerful counter-attack (反击)
  • Grants (给予) invincibility frames (无敌帧)
  • Recovers (恢复) some energy

Defensive Assist (防御支援)

When your active agent is about to be hit, you can switch to another agent to trigger a Defensive Assist (防御支援). The incoming (即将到来的) agent will parry (格挡) the attack and counter (反击).

Timing is Key (时机是关键):
Defensive Assist requires precise (精确的) timing. Switch too early or too late and you'll take damage instead (反而会受到伤害). Practice (练习) against easier enemies first!

Attack Types (攻击类型)

Each agent's attacks fall into (属于) one of three physical (物理) categories (类别):

Type (类型) Description (描述) Example Agents (示例角色)
Slash (斩击) Cutting attacks (切割攻击), usually swords/blades Ellen Joe, Miyabi
Strike (打击) Blunt force (钝击), hammers/fists Koleda, Ben
Pierce (穿刺) Penetrating (穿透) attacks, guns/spears Zhu Yuan, Billy

Energy System (能量系统)

Each agent has an Energy (能量) bar that fills through combat. When full, you can unleash (释放) their Ultimate (终结技) ability.

Ways to Gain Energy (获取能量的方式)

  • Dealing damage (造成伤害)
  • Perfect dodges (完美闪避)
  • Chain attacks (连携攻击)
  • Certain skills and passives (某些技能和被动)
  • Support agent buffs (辅助角色增益)

Combat Tips for Beginners (新手战斗技巧)

  1. Don't button mash (不要乱按): Learn your agent's combo timing (连招节奏) for optimal (最佳) damage.
  2. Watch enemy tells (观察敌人预兆): Most attacks have visual cues (视觉提示) before they land.
  3. Use the whole team (使用整个队伍): Switching agents is free and provides (提供) tactical (战术) advantages.
  4. Prioritize (优先) stunning: A stunned enemy takes much more damage. Focus on filling the Daze meter.
  5. Save ultimates for bosses (为首领保留终结技): Don't waste (浪费) your ultimate on weak enemies.
