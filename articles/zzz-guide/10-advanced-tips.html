Advanced Tips & Meta (进阶技巧与元游戏)

Advanced | 10-12 min read

Combat Optimization (战斗优化)

Animation Canceling (动画取消)

Animation canceling (动画取消) allows you to skip (跳过) recovery frames (恢复帧) and increase DPS. Common techniques (技巧):

  • Dodge Cancel (闪避取消): Dodge immediately after an attack lands (攻击命中后立即闪避) to skip end lag (跳过收招)
  • Switch Cancel (切换取消): Switch agents mid-combo (连招中切换角色) to cancel animations
  • Jump Cancel (跳跃取消): Some agents can jump to cancel certain moves (某些角色可以跳跃取消特定动作)
Practice Tip (练习技巧):
Go to training mode (训练模式) and practice cancels on a dummy (木桩). Watch your DPS numbers (观察DPS数值) to see improvement (提升).

I-Frames & Timing (无敌帧与时机)

I-frames (无敌帧) are moments of invincibility (无敌时刻) during certain actions:

  • Perfect Dodge (完美闪避): Generous (宽裕的) i-frames
  • Ultimate activation (终结技激活): Brief (短暂的) invincibility
  • Chain Attack startup (连携攻击启动): Some i-frames during transition (过渡期间有一些无敌帧)

Stat Optimization (属性优化)

CRIT Ratio (暴击比例)

The ideal (理想的) CRIT Rate to CRIT DMG ratio (比例) is approximately (大约) 1:2.

CRIT Rate (暴击率) Target CRIT DMG (目标暴伤)
50% 100%
60% 120%
70% 140%
80% 160%
Diminishing Returns (收益递减):
After ~70% CRIT Rate, additional (额外的) CRIT Rate has diminishing (递减的) value. Shift focus (转移重点) to CRIT DMG or ATK%.

ATK vs Elemental DMG (攻击力 vs 元素伤害)

Balance (平衡) is key. Too much of one stat leads to diminishing returns (收益递减).

  • Base ATK from W-Engine is multiplied (乘以) by ATK%
  • Elemental DMG% is additive (加法) with other DMG bonuses
  • Aim for (目标) ~110-130% ATK bonus and ~40-60% Elemental DMG

Resource Management (资源管理)

Polychrome Priority (菲林优先级)

  1. Limited Banners (限定卡池): New S-Rank agents you want (你想要的新S级角色)
  2. Signature W-Engines (专属W引擎): Only for your main DPS (仅限主输出), optional (可选的)
  3. Standard Banner (常驻卡池): Low priority (低优先级), use free pulls only (仅使用免费抽)

Stamina Usage (体力使用)

  • Daily priorities (每日优先): Routine Cleanup for Drive Discs
  • Weekly (每周): Hollow Zero bounties (悬赏)
  • Events (活动): Always clear (总是清空) event shops first

Meta Considerations (元游戏考量)

Current Meta Trends (当前元游戏趋势)

The meta (元游戏) evolves (演变) with each update. General observations (一般观察):

  • Anomaly teams (异常队) are increasingly (越来越) viable (可行的) with new agents
  • Stun remains essential (击破仍然必要): Even in Anomaly teams, Daze matters (即使在异常队中，失衡也很重要)
  • Supports are underrated (辅助被低估): Good supports multiply (成倍增加) team damage significantly (显著地)

Future-Proofing (面向未来)

Tips for long-term (长期) account health (账号健康):

  1. Diversify (多样化) elements: Build at least one team per element (每个元素至少组建一支队伍)
  2. Don't skip A-Ranks (不要跳过A级): Some A-Ranks are meta-relevant (某些A级与元游戏相关) (Nicole, Lucy, Anby)
  3. Save for reruns (为复刻存储): If you miss a limited agent (如果错过限定角色), they'll return (会复刻)

Common Mistakes to Avoid (要避免的常见错误)

1. Spreading Resources (分散资源)
Don't level every agent equally (不要平均升级每个角色). Focus on 6-8 core agents (专注于6-8个核心角色) first.
2. Ignoring Substats (忽视副词条)
A good substat roll (好的副词条) on an off-set piece (散件) beats (胜过) a bad 4-piece set bonus.
3. Chasing Perfect Gear (追求完美装备)
"Good enough" (足够好) clears all content. Perfect gear is for whales (完美装备是给大氪玩家的).
4. Neglecting Supports (忽视辅助)
A well-built support (培养好的辅助) increases team DPS more than marginal (边际的) DPS upgrades.

Final Tips (最终建议)

  1. Play what you enjoy (玩你喜欢的): Meta changes (元游戏会变), but fun is constant (但乐趣是恒定的)
  2. Join communities (加入社区): Discord, Reddit, and forums (论坛) have valuable (有价值的) resources
  3. Watch content creators (观看内容创作者): Learn advanced techniques (学习高级技巧) from experienced (有经验的) players
  4. Be patient (要有耐心): Gacha games reward (奖励) long-term (长期) commitment (投入)
