## Combat Optimization (战斗优化)

### Animation Canceling (动画取消)

Animation canceling (动画取消) allows you to
skip (跳过) recovery frames (恢复帧) and increase
DPS. Common techniques (技巧):

- **Dodge Cancel** (闪避取消):
  Dodge immediately after an attack lands (攻击命中后立即闪避) to skip end lag (跳过收招)
- **Switch Cancel** (切换取消):
  Switch agents mid-combo (连招中切换角色) to cancel animations
- **Jump Cancel** (跳跃取消):
  Some agents can jump to cancel certain moves (某些角色可以跳跃取消特定动作)

**Practice Tip (练习技巧):**  
Go to training mode (训练模式) and practice cancels on a dummy (木桩).
Watch your DPS numbers (观察DPS数值) to see improvement (提升).

### I-Frames & Timing (无敌帧与时机)

I-frames (无敌帧) are moments of invincibility
(无敌时刻) during certain actions:

- Perfect Dodge (完美闪避): Generous (宽裕的) i-frames
- Ultimate activation (终结技激活): Brief (短暂的) invincibility
- Chain Attack startup (连携攻击启动): Some i-frames during transition (过渡期间有一些无敌帧)

## Stat Optimization (属性优化)

### CRIT Ratio (暴击比例)

The ideal (理想的) CRIT Rate to CRIT DMG ratio (比例)
is approximately (大约) **1:2**.

| CRIT Rate(暴击率) | Target CRIT DMG(目标暴伤) |
| --- | --- |
| 50% | 100% |
| 60% | 120% |
| 70% | 140% |
| 80% | 160% |

**Diminishing Returns (收益递减):**  
After ~70% CRIT Rate, additional (额外的) CRIT Rate has diminishing
(递减的) value. Shift focus (转移重点) to CRIT DMG or ATK%.

### ATK vs Elemental DMG (攻击力 vs 元素伤害)

Balance (平衡) is key. Too much of one stat leads to
diminishing returns (收益递减).

- Base ATK from W-Engine is multiplied (乘以) by ATK%
- Elemental DMG% is additive (加法) with other DMG bonuses
- Aim for (目标) ~110-130% ATK bonus and ~40-60% Elemental DMG

## Resource Management (资源管理)

3. **Standard Banner** (常驻卡池):
   Low priority (低优先级), use free pulls only (仅使用免费抽)

## Meta Considerations (元游戏考量)

### Current Meta Trends (当前元游戏趋势)

The meta (元游戏) evolves (演变) with each update.
General observations (一般观察):

- **Anomaly teams** (异常队) are increasingly (越来越)
  viable (可行的) with new agents
- **Stun remains essential** (击破仍然必要):
  Even in Anomaly teams, Daze matters (即使在异常队中，失衡也很重要)
- **Supports are underrated** (辅助被低估):
  Good supports multiply (成倍增加) team damage significantly (显著地)

### Future-Proofing (面向未来)

Tips for long-term (长期) account health (账号健康):

1. **Diversify (多样化) elements**:
   Build at least one team per element (每个元素至少组建一支队伍)
2. **Don't skip A-Ranks** (不要跳过A级):
   Some A-Ranks are meta-relevant (某些A级与元游戏相关) (Nicole, Lucy, Anby)
3. **Save for reruns** (为复刻存储):
   If you miss a limited agent (如果错过限定角色), they'll return (会复刻)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid (要避免的常见错误)

**1. Spreading Resources (分散资源)**  
Don't level every agent equally (不要平均升级每个角色).
Focus on 6-8 core agents (专注于6-8个核心角色) first.

**2. Ignoring Substats (忽视副词条)**  
A good substat roll (好的副词条) on an off-set piece (散件)
beats (胜过) a bad 4-piece set bonus.

**3. Chasing Perfect Gear (追求完美装备)**  
"Good enough" (足够好) clears all content.
Perfect gear is for whales (完美装备是给大氪玩家的).

**4. Neglecting Supports (忽视辅助)**  
A well-built support (培养好的辅助) increases team DPS more than
marginal (边际的) DPS upgrades.
