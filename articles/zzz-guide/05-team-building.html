Team Building Guide (组队指南)

Intermediate | 10-12 min read

Team Building Fundamentals (组队基础)

Building an effective (有效的) team in ZZZ requires understanding synergy (协同效应) between agents. A well-constructed (构建良好的) team multiplies (成倍增加) each member's effectiveness (效果).

The Three Pillars (三大支柱)

  1. Damage (伤害): Someone needs to deal the damage
  2. Enabling (辅助): Create opportunities (机会) for damage
  3. Sustain (续航): Keep the team alive (存活) and fighting

Team Archetypes (队伍原型)

1. Standard DPS Team (标准输出队)

The most common (常见的) and beginner-friendly (新手友好的) composition (组合).

Structure (结构)

Attack DPS Stun Agent Support

How it works (运作方式):

  1. Stun agent fills enemy Daze meter (击破角色填满失衡条)
  2. Support buffs the team (辅助增益队伍)
  3. Attack DPS unleashes damage during stun (强攻在眩晕期间释放伤害)

2. Disorder Team (紊乱队)

Focuses on triggering (触发) Disorder for sustained (持续的) damage.

Structure (结构)

Anomaly A Anomaly B Support/Flex

Key requirement (关键要求): Two anomaly agents must have different attributes (不同属性) to trigger Disorder!

3. Hypercarry Team (超级主C队)

All resources focused on (集中于) one powerful carry (主C).

Structure (结构)

Main DPS Buffer 1 Buffer 2

Best for (最适合): Highly invested (高投入的) S-Rank attackers like Ellen Joe or Miyabi

4. Quickswap Team (速切队)

Rapidly (快速地) switches between agents for constant (持续的) damage.

Structure (结构)

Sub-DPS 1 Sub-DPS 2 Sub-DPS 3

Playstyle (玩法): Use each agent's burst (爆发技能), then switch. No dedicated (专门的) support needed.

Faction Synergies (阵营协同)

Some Bangboo provide bonuses (加成) when your team has agents from the same faction (阵营). This can significantly (显著地) boost (提升) team performance.

Strategy (策略): Lycaon stuns, Soukaku buffs Ice DMG, Ellen deals massive (大量) burst damage. All Victoria faction for Bangboo bonus.

Strategy (策略): Qingyi's fast stun, Nicole's DEF shred (防御削减), Zhu Yuan's gun barrage (枪弹连射).

Strategy (策略): Burnice applies Burn, Jane triggers Disorder with Physical. Lucy buffs ATK. Constant (持续的) Disorder procs (触发).

Team Building Tips (组队技巧)

Tip 1: Element Matching (元素匹配)
Match your DPS element with support buffs. Ice DPS + Ice DMG buffer = maximum damage. (让输出元素与辅助增益匹配。冰输出+冰伤增益=最大伤害)
Tip 2: Don't Neglect Stun (不要忽视击破)
Even the best DPS needs stun windows. Invest in (投资) at least one good Stun agent.
Common Mistake (常见错误):
Running 3 DPS agents without stun or support. You'll struggle (挣扎) against bosses who don't get stunned easily (不容易被眩晕).

Bangboo Selection (邦布选择)

Choose Bangboo based on (基于):

  • Faction bonus (阵营加成): If running same-faction team
  • Element match (元素匹配): Bangboo element should complement (补充) your DPS
  • Utility (实用性): Some Bangboo provide healing or buffs
