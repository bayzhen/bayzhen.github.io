## What are Bangboo? (什么是邦布?)

Bangboo (邦布) are semi-intelligent (半智能的),
rabbit-like (兔子般的) robots unique to New Eridu. Originally designed
(最初设计) to help residents (居民) evacuate
(撤离) during Hollow disasters (空洞灾害),
they've evolved into (演变成) versatile (多功能的)
combat companions (战斗伙伴).

## Bangboo in Combat (战斗中的邦布)

In gameplay, Combat Bangboo (战斗邦布)
function as obtainable (可获得的) companion units. Each team can
have one Bangboo that provides (提供):

- **Passive abilities** (被动能力): Always active buffs (始终激活的增益)
- **Active skills** (主动技能): Triggered (触发的) during combat
- **Bangboo Chain Attack** (邦布连携攻击): Special attack with its own cooldown (独立冷却的特殊攻击)

**Key Point (要点):**  
Bangboo Chain Attacks have their own cooldown and don't count toward (不计入)
the regular chain attack limit! This means extra (额外的) damage during stun windows.

## Bangboo Attributes (邦布属性)

Each Bangboo has:

- **Rarity** (稀有度): A-Rank or S-Rank
- **Attribute** (属性): Physical, Ice, Fire, Electric, or Ether
- **Faction** (阵营): Some belong to specific factions (某些属于特定阵营)

## Faction Bonuses (阵营加成)

Many Bangboo provide enhanced (增强的) effects when your team has
agents from a specific faction. This is called Faction Resonance
(阵营共鸣).

| Condition(条件) | Bonus Type(加成类型) |
| --- | --- |
| 2 agents from same faction(2个同阵营角色) | Basic faction bonus(基础阵营加成) |
| 3 agents from same faction(3个同阵营角色) | Enhanced faction bonus(增强阵营加成) |

## Obtaining Bangboo (获取邦布)

### Signal Search (信号搜索)

Use Boopons (邦布券) on the permanent
(常驻的) "An Outstanding Partner" channel (卓越伙伴频道).

**S-Rank Selector (S级选择器):**  
This channel includes an S-Rank selector (S级选择器) that guarantees
(保证) any S-Rank Bangboo of your choice! Save for the one that
fits (适合) your main team.

### Other Sources (其他来源)

- **Flagship Events** (旗舰活动): Limited-time (限时) exclusive Bangboo
- **Logistics Shop** (后勤商店): Some Bangboo like Luckyboo and Knightboo
- **Story Rewards** (剧情奖励): Free Bangboo from progression (推进获得免费邦布)

## Bangboo Selection Guide (邦布选择指南)

### Priority Factors (优先因素)

1. **Faction Match** (阵营匹配):
   If running a same-faction team, pick the matching Bangboo (如果使用同阵营队伍，选择匹配的邦布)
2. **Element Match** (元素匹配):
   Bangboo element should complement (补充) your DPS
3. **Utility** (实用性):
   Some Bangboo provide healing, shields, or unique buffs (某些邦布提供治疗、护盾或独特增益)

### Team Examples (队伍示例)

| Team Type(队伍类型) | Recommended Bangboo(推荐邦布) |
| --- | --- |
| Ellen Ice Team(艾莲冰队) | Butler (Victoria bonus)(管家-维多利亚加成) |
| Zhu Yuan Physical(朱鸢物理队) | Resonaboo or Officer Cui(共鸣邦布或崔警官) |
| Fire Anomaly(火异常队) | Red Moccus (Calydon bonus)(红莫库斯-卡吕冬加成) |
| Mixed Team(混合队) | Amillion (general use)(阿米利恩-通用) |

**Don't Neglect Bangboo! (不要忽视邦布!)**  
A well-chosen (精心选择的) Bangboo can significantly (显著地)
boost your team's performance. Level them up (升级它们) alongside
(与...一起) your agents!
