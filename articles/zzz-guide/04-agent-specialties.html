Agent Specialties & Roles (角色专精与定位)

Intermediate | 8-10 min read

Understanding Specialties (理解专精)

Every agent in ZZZ has a Specialty (专精) that defines (定义) their role in combat. There are currently 6 specialties, each with distinct (独特的) strengths (优势) and playstyles (玩法风格).

Why Specialties Matter (为什么专精重要):
- W-Engine effects only activate (激活) when specialty matches (匹配)
- Determines (决定) optimal (最佳) stat priorities (属性优先级)
- Guides team composition (指导队伍组成) decisions

The Six Specialties (六大专精)

Attack (强攻)

Role: Primary (主要) damage dealer (输出者)
Focus: Maximizing (最大化) damage during stun windows (眩晕窗口期)

Attack agents are your main DPS (主C/主输出). They have high damage multipliers (高伤害倍率) but often rely on (依赖) teammates to create openings (创造机会).

Key Stats (关键属性): ATK, CRIT Rate, CRIT DMG, Elemental DMG Bonus

Notable Agents (代表角色): S Ellen Joe, Zhu Yuan, Miyabi A Billy, Corin, Anton

Stun (击破)

Role: Daze accumulator (失衡积累者)
Focus: Filling enemy Daze meters quickly (快速填满敌人失衡条)

Stun agents have high Impact (冲击力) stats and skills that rapidly (快速地) build Daze. Essential (必要的) for enabling (使能够) Attack agents to deal maximum damage.

Key Stats (关键属性): Impact, ATK, Daze Multiplier

Notable Agents (代表角色): S Lycaon, Qingyi, Lighter A Koleda, Anby

Anomaly (异常)

Role: Debuff (减益) applicator (施加者)
Focus: Triggering attribute anomalies and Disorder (触发属性异常和紊乱)

Anomaly agents excel at (擅长) applying status effects (状态效果). They can deal significant (显著的) damage through Disorder combos (紊乱连招).

Key Stats (关键属性): Anomaly Proficiency, Anomaly Mastery, ATK

Notable Agents (代表角色): S Jane Doe, Burnice, Yanagi A Grace, Piper

Support (支援)

Role: Team buffer (团队增益者) and enabler (辅助者)
Focus: Enhancing (增强) teammate performance (表现)

Support agents provide buffs (增益), healing (治疗), or utility (实用功能). They amplify (放大) the team's overall (整体) effectiveness (效果).

Key Stats (关键属性): Varies by agent - often HP, Energy Regen

Notable Agents (代表角色): S Rina, Caesar, Soukaku A Nicole, Lucy

Defense (防护)

Role: Tank (坦克) and protector (保护者)
Focus: Absorbing (吸收) damage and shielding (护盾) teammates

Defense agents have high HP and DEF. They can take hits (承受攻击) for the team and often provide shields (护盾) or damage reduction (伤害减免).

Key Stats (关键属性): HP, DEF, Impact

Notable Agents (代表角色): S Caesar King A Ben Bigger

Rupture (破裂)

Role: Sustained (持续) damage dealer
Focus: Dealing consistent (稳定的) damage over time

Added in Version 2.0, Rupture agents specialize in (专精于) dealing steady (稳定的) damage without relying heavily on (不过度依赖) stun windows.

Key Stats (关键属性): ATK, CRIT, Elemental DMG

Notable Agents (代表角色): S (Newer agents)

Team Role Distribution (队伍角色分配)

A balanced (平衡的) team typically (通常) includes:

Slot (位置) Recommended Role (推荐角色) Purpose (目的)
Slot 1 Attack / Anomaly Main damage source (主要伤害来源)
Slot 2 Stun / Defense Create openings / Protection (创造机会/保护)
Slot 3 Support / Sub-DPS Buff team / Secondary damage (增益团队/副输出)

Specialty Synergies (专精协同)

Classic Combo: Stun + Attack (经典组合：击破+强攻)

The most fundamental (基础的) synergy. Stun agent fills Daze, then Attack agent unleashes (释放) damage during stun window.

Disorder Combo: Anomaly + Anomaly (紊乱组合：异常+异常)

Two different-element Anomaly agents trigger Disorder repeatedly (反复) for massive (大量) sustained damage.

Hypercarry: Attack + Support + Support (超级主C：强攻+支援+支援)

Focus all resources (资源) on buffing one powerful Attack agent. Requires (需要) well-built (培养好的) supports.

Beginner Tip (新手提示):
Start with the Stun + Attack combo. It's straightforward (直接的) and effective (有效的). As you get more agents, experiment (尝试) with other compositions (组合)!

Choosing Your Main (选择你的主力)

When deciding which agents to invest in (投资培养), consider:

  1. Playstyle preference (玩法偏好): Do you like big burst (爆发) or sustained damage?
  2. Available resources (可用资源): S-Rank agents need more investment (投入)
  3. Team gaps (队伍空缺): Fill roles you're missing (缺少的)
  4. Future banners (未来卡池): Save for agents that complement (补充) your roster (阵容)
