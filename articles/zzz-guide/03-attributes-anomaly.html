Attributes & Anomaly System (属性与异常系统)

Beginner | 8-10 min read

The Five Core Attributes (五大核心属性)

Every agent in ZZZ deals damage of a specific Attribute (属性). Understanding attributes is crucial (至关重要的) for team building and exploiting (利用) enemy weaknesses (弱点).

Attribute (属性) Color Anomaly Effect (异常效果)
Physical (物理) Gray Assault (强袭) + Flinch (畏缩)
Fire (火) Red Burn (燃烧)
Ice (冰) Blue Freeze (冻结) + Shatter (碎裂)
Electric (电) Purple Shock (感电)
Ether (以太) Teal Corruption (侵蚀)

Special Attributes (特殊属性)

Some agents have special sub-attributes (特殊子属性) that are variants (变体) of the core five. These have unique (独特的) properties (特性).

Special Attribute Base (基础) Unique Property (独特特性) Agent
Frost (霜) Ice Frostbite lasts 20s (vs Ice's 10s) (冻伤持续20秒) Miyabi
Honed Edge (锋锐) Physical +50% Miasma purification (瘴气净化+50%) Ye Shunguang
Auric Ink (金墨) Ether Separate anomaly gauge (独立异常条) Yixuan
Key Point (要点):
Special attributes maintain (保持) separate anomaly buildup gauges (独立的异常积累条). This means they can trigger Disorder (紊乱) independently (独立地) from their base attribute!

Anomaly Buildup (异常积累)

When agents deal attribute damage, they inflict (造成) Anomaly Buildup (异常积累) on enemies. Once the buildup reaches a threshold (阈值), it triggers (触发) an Attribute Anomaly (属性异常).

Anomaly Proficiency (异常精通): A stat that increases (增加) both the rate of anomaly buildup and the damage dealt by anomaly effects.

Attribute Anomaly Effects (属性异常效果)

Physical - Assault & Flinch (强袭与畏缩)

  • Assault (强袭): Interrupts (打断) the enemy and deals massive (大量) Physical damage
  • Flinch (畏缩): Increases Daze taken (增加受到的失衡值) by the enemy

Fire - Burn (燃烧)

  • Deals continuous (持续的) Fire damage over time (持续伤害)
  • Can interrupt (打断) enemies weak to Fire
  • Stacks (叠加) with multiple applications (多次施加)

Ice - Freeze & Shatter (冻结与碎裂)

  • Freeze (冻结): Completely immobilizes (完全定住) the enemy
  • Shatter (碎裂): Deals Ice damage when freeze ends
  • Frostbite (冻伤): Increases CRIT damage taken (增加受到的暴击伤害)

Electric - Shock (感电)

  • Deals intermittent (间歇性的) Electric damage when the enemy is attacked
  • Can interrupt enemies weak to Electric
  • Excellent (极好的) for sustained (持续的) damage

Ether - Corruption (侵蚀)

  • Deals extra Ether damage when the enemy is attacked
  • Can interrupt enemies weak to Ether
  • Synergizes (协同) well with fast-hitting (快速攻击的) agents

Disorder Damage (紊乱伤害)

Disorder (紊乱) is a powerful mechanic (机制) that occurs when you apply a different attribute anomaly to an enemy that already has one active (已激活的).

How Disorder Works (紊乱如何运作):
1. Enemy has Anomaly A active (敌人有异常A激活)
2. You trigger Anomaly B (你触发异常B)
3. Anomaly A is replaced (异常A被替换)
4. Disorder damage is dealt based on Anomaly A (基于异常A造成紊乱伤害)

Disorder Damage Multipliers (紊乱伤害倍率)

Time Since Anomaly A (异常A触发后时间) Multiplier (倍率)
Immediately (立即) 450%
After some time (一段时间后) Higher (varies) (更高，视情况而定)
Disorder Strategy (紊乱策略):
Anomaly agents (异常型角色) excel at (擅长) triggering Disorder. Build teams with 2 different attributes to maximize (最大化) Disorder damage!

Practical Application (实际应用)

Team Composition Example (队伍组成示例)

A classic (经典的) Disorder team might include:

  • Fire Anomaly agent (火异常角色): Applies Burn
  • Electric Anomaly agent (电异常角色): Triggers Disorder with Shock
  • Support (辅助): Buffs (增益) Anomaly Proficiency

Key Stats for Anomaly Builds (异常流派关键属性)

Stat (属性) Effect (效果)
Anomaly Proficiency (异常精通) Increases buildup rate and anomaly damage
Anomaly Mastery (异常掌控) Increases anomaly damage specifically (专门增加异常伤害)
ATK (攻击力) Base for anomaly damage calculation (异常伤害计算基础)
