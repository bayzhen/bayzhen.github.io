## What is Zenless Zone Zero? (什么是绝区零?)

Zenless Zone Zero (ZZZ) is a free-to-play (免费游玩)
action RPG developed by HoYoverse (米哈游).
Released on July 4, 2024, it's available on PC, PlayStation 5, iOS, and Android.

The game features fast-paced hack-and-slash (砍杀) combat
with stylish (时尚的) urban aesthetics (美学) and
a unique roguelike (肉鸽) exploration system.

## The Setting: New Eridu (背景设定：新艾利都)

The story takes place in New Eridu (新艾利都),
humanity's last urban civilization (城市文明)
in a post-apocalyptic (后启示录/末日后) world.

**Hollows** (空洞): Supernatural (超自然的)
dimensional (维度的) rifts (裂缝) that have
devastated (摧毁) most of humanity. They spawn (生成)
hostile (敌对的) creatures called Ethereals (以太怪物).

**Ethereals** (以太怪物): Hostile creatures that emerge
(出现) from Hollows. They are the primary (主要的)
enemies you'll fight throughout (贯穿) the game.

## Main Characters (主要角色)

You play as the Phaethon (绳匠) - either
**Belle** (female) or **Wise** (male). They are siblings
(兄妹/姐弟) who run a video rental store called "Random Play" as a
cover (掩护).

Their real job is being Proxies (代理人) -
specialists (专家) who guide people through Hollows using a device
called the Hollow Deep Dive System (空洞深潜系统).

## Major Factions (主要阵营)

Agents (代理人/角色) in ZZZ belong to various factions
(阵营). Understanding factions is important for team building
(组队) as some Bangboo (邦布) provide faction-specific
(阵营特定的) bonuses.

| Faction(阵营) | Description(描述) |
| --- | --- |
| Cunning Hares(狡兔屋) | The protagonist's(主角的)faction. Includes Belle, Wise, and Phaethon. |
| Victoria Housekeeping(维多利亚家政) | Elite(精英)maids and butlers(管家). Features Ellen Joe, Von Lycaon. |
| Belobog Heavy Industries(白祇重工) | Industrial(工业的)corporation(公司). Includes Koleda, Grace, Anton. |
| CIRS(刑侦特响科) | Criminal Investigation(刑事调查)Special Response Team. Zhu Yuan, Qingyi, Jane Doe. |
| Sons of Calydon(卡吕冬之子) | Biker gang(摩托车帮派). Caesar King, Burnice, Lighter. |
| Obol Squad(奥波勒斯小队) | Military(军事)unit. Soldier 11, Trigger. |
| Section 6(六分局) | Hollow Special Operations(空洞特别行动). Lycaon, Rina. |

## Core Gameplay Loop (核心游戏循环)

The main gameplay consists of (由...组成):

1. **Story Progression** (剧情推进):
   Follow the main narrative (叙事) through chapters.
2. **Combat Missions** (战斗任务):
   Enter Hollows to fight Ethereals and bosses.
3. **Character Building** (角色培养):
   Level up agents, equip Drive Discs (驱动盘) and W-Engines (W引擎).
4. **Exploration** (探索):
   Explore New Eridu, interact with NPCs (非玩家角色), complete side quests (支线任务).
5. **Endgame Content** (终局内容):
   Challenge Shiyu Defense (式舆防卫战), Deadly Assault (致命突袭), and Hollow Zero (零号空洞).

## Gacha System: Signal Search (抽卡系统：信号搜索)

ZZZ uses a gacha (抽卡) system called
**Signal Search** (信号搜索) to obtain (获得)
new agents and W-Engines.

**Rarity Tiers (稀有度等级):**  
- **S-Rank** (S级): Highest rarity (最高稀有度), 0.6% base rate (基础概率)  
- **A-Rank** (A级): Lower rarity, easier to obtain (更容易获得)

**Pity System (保底系统):**  
The game has a pity (保底) system.
You're guaranteed (保证) an S-Rank within 90 pulls (抽).
Soft pity (软保底) starts around 75 pulls with increased rates (提高概率).
