Drive Discs Equipment (驱动盘装备)

Intermediate | 10-12 min read

What are Drive Discs? (什么是驱动盘?)

Drive Discs (驱动盘) are equipment items that boost (提升) your agents' stats. Think of them like artifacts (圣遗物) in other gacha games. Each agent can equip (装备) up to 6 Drive Discs.

Slot System (槽位系统)

Each slot has specific (特定的) main stats available (可用的). Understanding this is crucial (至关重要的) for optimization (优化).

1
HP (Flat) (固定生命)
2
ATK (Flat) (固定攻击)
3
DEF (Flat) (固定防御)
4
HP% / ATK% / DEF% / CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG / Anomaly Prof.
5
HP% / ATK% / DEF% / PEN Ratio / Element DMG%
6
HP% / ATK% / DEF% / Impact% / Anomaly Mastery / Energy Regen%
Key Insight (关键洞察):
Slots 1-3 have fixed (固定的) main stats. Slots 4-6 are where you optimize (优化) for your agent's needs!

Rarity Tiers (稀有度等级)

Rarity (稀有度) Max Level (最高等级) Max Sub Stats (最多副词条)
B-Rank 9 2
A-Rank 12 3
S-Rank 15 4
Tip (提示):
Always aim for (追求) S-Rank Drive Discs for your main DPS. A-Rank is acceptable (可接受的) for supports early on (早期).

Set Bonuses (套装效果)

Drive Discs belong to (属于) specific sets. Equipping multiple pieces from the same set grants (给予) bonuses:

  • 2-piece bonus (2件套效果): Usually a stat boost (属性提升)
  • 4-piece bonus (4件套效果): More powerful effect (更强效果), often conditional (有条件的)
Important (重要):
There are NO 6-piece set effects (没有6件套效果)! Common setup (常见搭配): 4-piece + 2-piece from different sets.

Sub Stats Priority (副词条优先级)

For DPS Agents (输出角色)

  1. CRIT Rate (暴击率) - until ~60%
  2. CRIT DMG (暴击伤害)
  3. ATK% (攻击力%)
  4. Flat ATK (固定攻击)

For Anomaly Agents (异常角色)

  1. Anomaly Proficiency (异常精通)
  2. ATK% (攻击力%)
  3. CRIT stats (暴击属性) - for some agents

For Stun Agents (击破角色)

  1. Impact (冲击力)
  2. ATK% (攻击力%)
  3. Energy Regen (能量恢复)

Farming Locations (刷取地点)

  • Music Store (唱片店): Tune Master Copies (调谐母带) for specific sets
  • Routine Cleanup (日常清理): Random drops (随机掉落)
  • Hollow Zero (零号空洞): Additional sources (额外来源)
Farming Tip (刷取技巧):
Focus on (专注于) one set at a time. Getting good sub stats takes time (获得好副词条需要时间). Don't spread (分散) your resources too thin (太薄)!
