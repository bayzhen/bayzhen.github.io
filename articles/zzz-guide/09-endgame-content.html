Endgame Content (终局内容)

Advanced | 12-15 min read

Endgame Overview (终局概述)

Once you've progressed (推进) through the main story and built (培养) your agents, ZZZ offers several endgame modes (终局模式) to test your skills and earn premium (高级) rewards.

Shiyu Defense (式舆防卫战)

Overview (概述)

Unlock: Inter-Knot Level 20 (解锁：绳网等级20)

A periodic (周期性的) military operation (军事行动) where you defend (防守) Shiyu Pillars (式舆柱) within Hollow Zero. This is ZZZ's main Abyss-like (深渊类) content.

Node Types (节点类型)

Node Type (节点类型) Description (描述) Reset (重置)
Stable Nodes (稳定节点) Permanent (永久的) stages, unlock progressively (逐步解锁) Never
Disputed Nodes (争议节点) Added in v1.1, sequential (顺序的) unlock Never
Critical Nodes (关键节点) Rotating (轮换的) content, main reward source (主要奖励来源) Every 15 days

Rewards (奖励)

  • Polychrome (菲林): Premium currency (高级货币) for gacha
  • Agent copies (角色副本): For Mindscape Cinema (影院) upgrades
  • Achievements (成就): Titles and cosmetics (称号和装饰)
Strategy (策略):
Build two teams (组建两支队伍) for Shiyu Defense! Each side requires (需要) a separate (独立的) team. Plan your roster (阵容) accordingly (相应地).

Deadly Assault (致命突袭)

Overview (概述)

Unlock: Inter-Knot Level 40 + Shiyu Defense unlocked (解锁：绳网等级40+式舆防卫战已解锁)

A high-risk (高风险) enemy simulation (模拟) training system introduced (引入) in Version 1.4. Features three challenging (具有挑战性的) bosses per rotation (每轮换).

Key Mechanics (关键机制)

  • No Repeats (不可重复): Agents and Bangboo used cannot be repeated (使用过的角色和邦布不能重复使用) within a session
  • Revealed Weaknesses (显示弱点): Enemy weaknesses and resistances (抗性) are shown beforehand (事先)
  • Scaling Difficulty (递增难度): Enemies get harder (更难) as their HP decreases (减少)
  • Buff Selection (增益选择): Choose specific buffs before each battle (每场战斗前选择特定增益)

Rewards (奖励)

Completing all 9 stars yields (获得):

  • 300 Polychrome (菲林)
  • 720 Investigation Merits (调查功勋)
  • 225,000 Denny (丁尼)

Schedule (时间表)

Rotates (轮换) every two weeks, alternating (交替) with Shiyu Defense: Critical Node.

Hollow Zero (零号空洞)

Overview (概述)

Unlock: Story progression (解锁：剧情推进)

The largest known Hollow (已知最大的空洞), also called Limbo (边狱). A roguelike (肉鸽) exploration mode accessed through Scott Outpost (斯科特前哨站).

Game Modes (游戏模式)

Mode (模式) Description (描述)
Withered Domain (枯萎领域) Control Eous around Monitor Array (控制伊欧斯在监控阵列周围), manage Pressure and Corruptions (管理压力和腐化)
Lost Void (迷失虚空) Procedurally generated (程序生成的) events and combat encounters (战斗遭遇)
Arpeggio Fault (琶音断层) Pure Monitor Array combat (纯监控阵列战斗), manage HP and ATK stats

Key Systems (关键系统)

Resonia (共鸣石):
Collectible items (可收集物品) that grant combat or exploration buffs (战斗或探索增益). Purchase (购买) with Gear Coins from Bangboo Shops or find during exploration.
Bounty Commission (悬赏委托):
Weekly challenges (每周挑战) offering Shadow Crystal Data rewards (暗影晶体数据奖励). Complete 4 commissions per week across difficulty tiers (每周完成4个不同难度的委托).

Rewards (奖励)

  • Shadow Crystal Data (暗影晶体数据): For agent upgrades (角色升级用)
  • Dennies (丁尼)
  • Drive Discs (驱动盘)
  • W-Engine materials (W引擎材料)

Endgame Progression Tips (终局进阶技巧)

  1. Build Two Teams First (先组建两支队伍): Shiyu Defense requires two separate teams. Don't spread (分散) resources too thin.
  2. Focus on Core Stats (专注核心属性): CRIT Rate ~60%, then CRIT DMG. Don't chase (追求) perfect substats early.
  3. Learn Enemy Patterns (学习敌人模式): Each boss has tells (预兆). Practice (练习) dodging and parrying.
  4. Use Buffs Wisely (明智使用增益): In Deadly Assault, match buffs to your team's strengths (将增益与队伍优势匹配).
  5. Don't Skip Hollow Zero (不要跳过零号空洞): Weekly bounties (每周悬赏) provide essential (必要的) upgrade materials.
Common Mistake (常见错误):
Trying to clear (通关) all endgame content immediately (立即). It's designed (设计) for well-built teams. Take your time (慢慢来) and improve gradually (逐步提升)!
