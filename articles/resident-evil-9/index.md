## About This Series

Resident Evil is one of gaming's most enduring franchises — over 30 years, dozens of games, and more than 150 million copies sold. Resident Evil 9 is expected to push the series into its boldest territory yet. This series explores the franchise, the design philosophy behind survival horror, and what makes RE9 worth paying attention to.

Articles are written in English at CET-6 level, with inline annotations for difficult vocabulary. Each piece is 300–500 words — short enough to read (or copy out by hand) in a single sitting.

## What You'll Find

- **Franchise History** — How Resident Evil was born, nearly died, and reinvented itself three times
- **Survival Horror Design** — Why limited ammo and slow movement are features, not bugs
- **RE9: A New Direction** — What the latest entry promises and why fans are excited
