Every new Resident Evil game arrives carrying the same question: which version of itself will the series choose to be this time?

## A Larger World

Resident Evil 9 is reported to feature the most expansive world in the series' history. Not quite open world in the modern sense, but a dense, interconnected environment — closer to a Metroidvania than a linear corridor. Players will backtrack, discover new paths, and piece together a story that draws from decades of franchise lore.

This is a significant departure. Previous entries were tightly paced, often guiding players through a single location. RE9 seems willing to let players get genuinely lost.

> **Word Notes**
> - *expansive* /ɪkˈspænsɪv/ — 广阔的，宽广的。"An expansive landscape stretched before them."
> - *Metroidvania* — 类银河恶魔城游戏（强调探索、回溯的非线性关卡设计风格）。
> - *departure* /dɪˈpɑːtʃə/ — 背离，新的方向。"The film was a bold departure from his earlier work."

## Blending Perspectives

One of the most discussed design decisions is the camera. RE9 reportedly blends first-person and over-the-shoulder perspectives within the same game — switching depending on context. Intimate exploration in first-person; combat in third-person.

This is a calculated gamble. First-person horror (RE7, RE Village) delivers unmatched immersion. Third-person action (RE4 Remake) delivers clarity and control. Combining both risks satisfying neither — or could deliver the best of each.

> **Word Notes**
> - *immersion* /ɪˈmɜːʃən/ — 沉浸感。"VR technology enhances immersion in games."
> - *calculated gamble* — 经过权衡的冒险，有把握的赌注。"Launching the product early was a calculated gamble."

## What the Community Expects

Resident Evil fans are not easy to please. The fanbase is split between those who prefer the horror-focused early games and those who love the action-forward later entries. RE9 has to thread a needle.

What most agree on: the series should be scary again. RE Village leaned heavily into spectacle — enormous bosses, dramatic set pieces, a story that felt like an action film. RE9 seems to be pulling back toward tension and atmosphere. If the reports are accurate, this may be the most ambitious Resident Evil yet.

> **Word Notes**
> - *thread a needle* — 走钢丝，在对立要求之间找到平衡。"The politician had to thread a needle between two angry voter groups."
> - *spectacle* /ˈspektəkəl/ — 壮观的场面，视觉奇观。"The fireworks were quite a spectacle."
> - *set pieces* — 精心设计的高潮场面（游戏或影视中）。"The film's set pieces were visually stunning."

## Key Takeaways

- RE9 features a larger, interconnected world — a significant design shift for the series.
- A hybrid camera system blends first-person and third-person perspectives within the same game.
- The community hopes RE9 returns to genuine tension after RE Village's action-heavy approach.

*The franchise that reinvented itself every decade now faces its most ambitious reinvention yet. The only question is whether it sticks the landing.*
