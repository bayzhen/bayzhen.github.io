In 1996, a small Capcom team released a game about zombies in a mansion. Nobody expected it to last thirty years.

## The Beginning

Resident Evil — known in Japan as *Biohazard* — was not the first survival horror game, but it defined the genre. Players controlled a member of S.T.A.R.S., an elite police unit, trapped in a crumbling mansion filled with undead creatures. The atmosphere was suffocating. Resources were scarce. Death came fast.

The game sold over two million copies in its first year. A franchise was born.

> **Word Notes**
> - *suffocating* /ˈsʌfəkeɪtɪŋ/ — 令人窒息的，压抑的。"The heat in the room was suffocating."
> - *scarce* /skeəs/ — 稀缺的，不足的。"Food was scarce during the war."
> - *undead* — 不死的（通常指僵尸等）。"An army of undead creatures blocked the path."

## Near Death and Rebirth

By the early 2000s, the formula felt stale. Critics said the series was too slow, too rigid, too afraid to change. Capcom listened. Resident Evil 4, released in 2005, scrapped the fixed camera angles and introduced over-the-shoulder action gameplay. It was a radical reinvention — and one of the greatest games ever made.

But reinvention became a habit. RE7 in 2017 switched to first-person horror, stripping the series back to its terrifying roots. RE Village in 2021 added gothic atmosphere and a villain — Lady Dimitrescu — who became a cultural phenomenon overnight.

> **Word Notes**
> - *stale* /steɪl/ — 陈腐的，缺乏新意的。"The jokes felt stale after the third retelling."
> - *scrapped* /skræpt/ — 废弃，彻底抛弃。"They scrapped the original plan and started over."
> - *radical* /ˈrædɪkəl/ — 根本性的，激进的。"A radical change in strategy was needed."

## The Pattern

Resident Evil's secret is not horror. It is adaptability. Every time the series grows comfortable, it breaks itself apart and rebuilds. Critics call this inconsistency. Fans call it courage.

After thirty years and over 150 million copies sold, the franchise shows no signs of slowing down. Resident Evil 9 is next. And if history is any guide, it will surprise everyone.

## Key Takeaways

- Resident Evil defined survival horror in 1996 with atmosphere, limited resources, and terror.
- The series reinvented itself with RE4, RE7, and RE Village — each a bold departure from the previous entry.
- Adaptability, not formula, is what has kept the franchise alive for three decades.

*Some monsters refuse to die. The best franchises are built the same way.*
