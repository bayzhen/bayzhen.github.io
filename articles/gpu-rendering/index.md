## About This Series

Every frame you see on screen is the result of billions of calculations happening inside your GPU. This series starts with the fundamentals — why GPUs exist, how the rendering pipeline works, and what shaders do. Then it dives into Unreal Engine 5, exploring how one of the most powerful game engines implements these concepts in practice: its rendering architecture, material system, Lumen, Nanite, and optimization techniques.

## What You'll Find

### Part 1 — GPU Rendering Fundamentals

- **Article 1 — Why GPUs Exist** The parallel processing revolution that changed computing forever
- **Article 2 — The Rendering Pipeline** How vertices become pixels, step by step
- **Article 3 — Shaders** The programmable heart of every modern GPU
- **Article 4 — Textures and Lighting** How GPUs create visual realism on screen
- **Article 5 — Modern Rendering** Deferred shading, ray tracing, and what comes next

### Part 2 — Rendering in Unreal Engine

- **Article 6 — UE Rendering Architecture** How Unreal organizes its renderer: RHI, scene proxies, and the render thread
- **Article 7 — UE Materials** The material editor, shader compilation, and how artists control the GPU
- **Article 8 — Lumen and Nanite** UE5's breakthrough systems for global illumination and virtual geometry
- **Article 9 — UE Rendering Optimization** Draw calls, culling, profiling, and making frames fit in 16ms

## How to Use This Series

Each article is 300–500 words — short enough to copy out by hand in one sitting. Difficult words are annotated inline. Part 1 covers universal GPU knowledge; Part 2 applies it to Unreal Engine. Read in order for the full picture, or jump to any topic that interests you.

Start with [Why GPUs Exist](01-why-gpus-exist.html).
