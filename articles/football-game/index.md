## Series Overview

This series is designed for game developers who are new to football (soccer) and need to quickly build domain knowledge before joining a football game project. All articles are written in English with Chinese annotations for difficult vocabulary and complex sentence patterns.

### Reading Guide

- **Difficult vocabulary** is *italicized* with Chinese translation in parentheses
- **Complex sentences** are followed by a `> 句型解析:` block with Chinese explanation
- Terms marked **[Dev]** are game development specific
- Terms marked **[Adv]** are advanced vocabulary

### Content Structure

**Part I — Football Fundamentals** (Articles 01–03): Core football knowledge — rules, positions, and tactics.

**Part II — Game Design** (Articles 04–07): How football translates into game systems — genres, attributes, mechanics, and AI.

**Part III — Technical Implementation** (Articles 08–10): Engineering patterns — architecture, physics, and UI/UX.

**Part IV — Online & Abilities** (Article 12): Network architecture and Gameplay Ability System design for online football games.

**Part V — Reference** (Article 11): A comprehensive English-Chinese glossary of football and game development terminology.
