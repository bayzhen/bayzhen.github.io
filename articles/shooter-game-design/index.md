## Series Overview

This series explores the **system design** behind large-scale multiplayer third-person shooter (TPS) games. Every article focuses on *design thinking* — the "why" and "how" of each subsystem — rather than implementation code. The goal is to help game designers and engineers build a clear mental model of how modern shooters are structured.

All articles are written in CET-6 level English with Chinese annotations for difficult vocabulary and complex sentences.

### Reading Guide

- **Difficult vocabulary** is *italicized* with Chinese translation in parentheses
- **Complex sentences** are followed by a `> 句型解析:` block with Chinese explanation
- Terms marked **[Des]** are game design specific
- Terms marked **[Adv]** are advanced vocabulary

### Content Structure

**Part I — Foundation** (Articles 01–02): High-level architecture and the character system that sits at the heart of any shooter.

**Part II — Combat Core** (Articles 03–04): Weapon mechanics and the ability/effect framework that powers all combat interactions.

**Part III — Player Experience** (Articles 05–06): UI framework design and gameplay mode architecture that shape what players see and feel.

**Part IV — Intelligence & Connectivity** (Articles 07–08): AI design for bots and the network architecture that keeps everything in sync.

**Part V — Motion** (Article 09): Animation system design — how characters and weapons come alive through motion.
