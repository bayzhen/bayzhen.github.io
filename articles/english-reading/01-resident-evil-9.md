It has been over a quarter century since a zombie bit into an S.T.A.R.S. officer in a creaky mansion, and the world has never quite recovered.

## A Franchise Built on Fear

Resident Evil — known in Japan as *Biohazard* — is one of gaming's most iconic survival horror series. Since 1996, Capcom has sold over 150 million copies worldwide. That number is not just impressive; it is a testament to how deeply fear sells.

The formula is deceptively simple: limited resources, terrifying enemies, and a story dripping with conspiracies. Every time fans think the series has run its course, Capcom reinvents it.

> **Word Notes**
> - *testament* /ˈtestəmənt/ — 证明，见证。"This award is a testament to her hard work."
> - *dripping with* — 充满，洋溢着（形容词短语，偏口语）。"The speech was dripping with irony."
> - *run its course* — 走到尽头，自然终结。"The disease had run its course after two weeks."

## What Makes RE9 Different

Resident Evil 9 promises to push the series into darker, more ambitious territory. Reports suggest the game blends the first-person intimacy of RE7 and RE8 with the over-the-shoulder action that made RE4 legendary.

The setting? A vast, interconnected world — not quite open world, but far more expansive than anything the series has attempted before. Players piece together a story that ties up loose ends from decades of lore.

> **Word Notes**
> - *intimacy* /ˈɪntɪməsi/ — 亲密感；此处指第一人称视角带来的沉浸感。
> - *over-the-shoulder* — 越肩视角（第三人称摄像机跟随在角色肩后）。
> - *loose ends* — 未解决的悬念，遗留问题。"The finale tied up all the loose ends."

## Why Horror Endures

Horror games tap into something primal. The adrenaline rush of surviving — barely — against an impossible threat is addictive in a way calm, comfortable games never are. Studies suggest that controlled fear in safe environments actually relieves stress. Escaping a monster, it turns out, is therapeutic.

Resident Evil understood this before anyone had the words for it.

> **Word Notes**
> - *primal* /ˈpraɪməl/ — 原始的，本能的。"A primal fear of the dark."
> - *therapeutic* /ˌθerəˈpjuːtɪk/ — 有疗愈效果的。"Gardening can be therapeutic."

## Key Takeaways

- Resident Evil has reinvented itself multiple times and remains a global phenomenon.
- RE9 is expected to blend gameplay styles from across the series' history.
- Horror endures because it fulfills a deep, primal human need for controlled fear.

*Some monsters never die — and neither do the franchises built around them.*
