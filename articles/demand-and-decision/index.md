## About This Series

This series proposes a new academic discipline: Demand Science. Not philosophy, not business theory — a systematic, trainable framework that enables students to continuously produce high-quality demands and make sound decisions. Every principle here is distilled from real-world cases, not invented from thin air.

## What You'll Find

- **Why Demand Is a Science** — Historical cases reveal that great demands share universal patterns
- **The Demand Anatomy** — A structural model to decompose any need into analyzable components
- **The Quality Filter** — A scoring formula to separate gold from noise, tested against real products
- **Systematic Discovery** — Four repeatable methods to find demands without waiting for inspiration
- **Training the Demand Mind** — How to teach this discipline, from curriculum to graduation criteria

## How to Use This Series

Each article is 300–500 words — short enough to copy out by hand in one sitting. Difficult words are annotated inline. Read straight through, or jump to any article that interests you.

Start with [Why Demand Is a Science](01-why-demand-is-a-science.html).
