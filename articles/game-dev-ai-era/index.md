## About This Series

AI is changing everything — or so the headlines say. But what does it actually look like from inside a game studio? This series is written from the perspective of a senior Unreal Engine developer who has shipped real games, trained reinforcement learning models, and spent years thinking about where AI truly helps and where it falls short. If you work in game development and wonder what AI means for your career, this series is for you.

## What You'll Find

- **Article 1 — The AI Tool Explosion** What people are actually building with AI right now
- **Article 2 — Why Game Dev Resists Automation** The pipeline problem, context limits, and deep customization
- **Article 3 — Efficiency Without Lower Barriers** Why AI makes experts faster but doesn't replace expertise
- **Article 4 — The Missing Layer** What game engines need to truly integrate with LLMs
- **Article 5 — Finding Your Edge** How to position yourself as the person AI can't replace

## How to Use This Series

Each article is 300–500 words — short enough to copy out by hand in one sitting. Difficult words are annotated inline. Read straight through, or jump to any article that interests you.

Start with [The AI Tool Explosion](01-ai-tool-explosion.html).
