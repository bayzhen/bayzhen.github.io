## Product Sourcing Models

### 1. Dropshipping (代发货)

**How it works**: Supplier ships directly to customer when you receive an order.

**Pros**:
- Low startup cost ($100-500)
- No inventory risk
- Easy to test products
- Location independent

**Cons**:
- Lower profit margins (20-30%)
- Less control over quality
- Longer shipping times
- Supplier dependency

**Best for**: Testing ideas, beginners, low budget

> **Vocabulary**:
> - **dropshipping** /ˈdrɒpʃɪpɪŋ/ - 代发货
> - **dependency** /dɪˈpendənsi/ - 依赖

### 2. Wholesale (批发)

**How it works**: Buy products in bulk from manufacturers or distributors.

**Pros**:
- Higher margins (40-60%)
- Quality control
- Faster shipping
- Brand control

**Cons**:
- Upfront investment ($1000-10000)
- Inventory risk
- Storage needed
- Cash flow management

**Best for**: Proven products, scaling businesses

> **Vocabulary**:
> - **wholesale** /ˈhəʊlseɪl/ - 批发
> - **bulk** /bʌlk/ - 大量
> - **upfront** /ˌʌpˈfrʌnt/ - 预付的

### 3. Manufacturing (制造)

**How it works**: Create your own products with a manufacturer.

**Pros**:
- Unique products
- Highest margins (50-70%)
- Complete control
- Brand differentiation

**Cons**:
- High minimum orders (MOQ: 500-1000 units)
- Large investment ($5000-50000)
- Long lead times (2-6 months)
- Quality control challenges

**Best for**: Established brands, unique product ideas

> **Vocabulary**:
> - **MOQ** (Minimum Order Quantity) - 最小起订量
> - **lead time** - 交货时间
> - **differentiation** /ˌdɪfərenʃiˈeɪʃn/ - 差异化

### 4. Print-on-Demand (按需印刷)

**How it works**: Products printed/customized when ordered.

**Pros**:
- Zero inventory
- Easy customization
- Low risk
- Fast to launch

**Cons**:
- Limited product types (apparel, mugs, posters)
- Lower margins (30-40%)
- Quality varies by provider
- Limited branding options

**Best for**: Custom designs, artists, low-risk testing

## Finding Reliable Suppliers

### Dropshipping Suppliers

**AliExpress**:
- Pros: Huge selection, low prices
- Cons: Long shipping (15-45 days), quality varies
- Best for: Testing products

**Oberlo/DSers** (Shopify apps):
- Pros: Easy integration, automated ordering
- Cons: Same suppliers as competitors
- Best for: Beginners

**Spocket**:
- Pros: US/EU suppliers, faster shipping
- Cons: Higher prices, smaller selection
- Best for: Quality-focused stores

**CJ Dropshipping**:
- Pros: Quality control, private warehouses
- Cons: Requires vetting suppliers
- Best for: Serious dropshippers

> **Vocabulary**:
> - **vetting** /ˈvetɪŋ/ - 审查

### Wholesale Suppliers

**Alibaba**:
- Pros: Direct from manufacturers, low MOQs
- Cons: Quality control needed, communication challenges
- Tips: Order samples, use Trade Assurance

**Faire**:
- Pros: Curated brands, net-60 terms
- Cons: Higher prices, US-focused
- Best for: Boutique stores

**Wholesale Central**:
- Pros: Directory of US wholesalers
- Cons: Must verify legitimacy
- Best for: Finding niche suppliers

**Trade Shows**:
- Pros: Meet suppliers in person, negotiate deals
- Cons: Travel costs, time investment
- Examples: Canton Fair (China), ASD Market Week (US)

> **Vocabulary**:
> - **curated** /kjʊəˈreɪtɪd/ - 精心挑选的
> - **legitimacy** /lɪˈdʒɪtɪməsi/ - 合法性

### Print-on-Demand Providers

**Printful**:
- Best quality, higher prices
- 2-7 day production
- Integrates with Shopify

**Printify**:
- Multiple suppliers, competitive pricing
- Quality varies
- More product options

**Gooten**:
- Good for scaling
- Bulk discounts

## Negotiating with Suppliers

### Key Negotiation Points

**Price**:
```
Initial quote: $10/unit
Your response: "I'm planning to order regularly. 
What's your best price for 100 units?"
Possible outcome: $8.50/unit
```

**MOQ**:
```
Supplier: "500 unit minimum"
Your response: "I'm a new business testing the market. 
Can we start with 100 units?"
Possible outcome: 200 units at slightly higher price
```

**Payment Terms**:
```
Standard: 100% upfront
Negotiate: 50% deposit, 50% before shipping
Established relationship: Net-30 or Net-60
```

**Shipping**:
```
Ask: "Can you include shipping in the unit price?"
Or: "What's your best shipping rate to [country]?"
```

> **Vocabulary**:
> - **negotiation** /nɪˌɡəʊʃiˈeɪʃn/ - 谈判
> - **deposit** /dɪˈpɒzɪt/ - 定金

### Negotiation Tips

1. **Build relationship**: Don't just focus on price
2. **Show commitment**: Mention long-term plans
3. **Be respectful**: Suppliers remember difficult customers
4. **Know market rates**: Research before negotiating
5. **Start small**: Prove yourself before asking for terms

## Product Pricing Strategy

### Cost Calculation

```
Example: Yoga Mat

Product cost: $8.00
Shipping to you: $2.00
Packaging materials: $1.00
Platform fees (3%): $0.60
Payment processing (3%): $0.60
Marketing (30%): $6.00
---
Total cost: $18.20

Selling price: $29.99
Profit per unit: $11.79 (39% margin)
```

### Pricing Models

**Cost-Plus Pricing**:
```
Formula: Cost × Markup = Price
Example: $10 × 3 = $30
Simple but ignores market
```

**Value-Based Pricing**:
```
Price based on perceived value
Premium positioning
Example: Same product, better branding = higher price
```

**Competitive Pricing**:
```
Match or undercut competitors
Race to bottom risk
Better: Match price, add value
```

**Psychological Pricing**:
```
$29.99 vs $30.00 (charm pricing)
$99 vs $89 (prestige pricing)
$19.99 → $24.99 → $29.99 (good-better-best)
```

> **Vocabulary**:
> - **markup** /ˈmɑːkʌp/ - 加价
> - **perceived** /pəˈsiːvd/ - 感知的
> - **undercut** /ˌʌndəˈkʌt/ - 削价竞争

## Inventory Management

### Inventory Models

**Just-in-Time (JIT)**:
- Order only when needed
- Minimal storage costs
- Risk of stockouts
- Best for: Dropshipping, POD

**Safety Stock**:
- Keep buffer inventory
- Prevents stockouts
- Ties up capital
- Best for: Wholesale, popular items

**Seasonal Stocking**:
- Build inventory before peak season
- Requires forecasting
- Higher risk
- Best for: Seasonal products

> **Vocabulary**:
> - **stockout** /ˈstɒkaʊt/ - 缺货
> - **buffer** /ˈbʌfə/ - 缓冲
> - **forecasting** /ˈfɔːkɑːstɪŋ/ - 预测

### Inventory Metrics

**Inventory Turnover**:
```
Formula: Cost of Goods Sold ÷ Average Inventory
Example: $50,000 ÷ $10,000 = 5 times per year
Good: 4-6 times/year (depends on industry)
```

**Days of Inventory**:
```
Formula: 365 ÷ Inventory Turnover
Example: 365 ÷ 5 = 73 days
Target: 30-90 days
```

**Reorder Point**:
```
Formula: (Daily Sales × Lead Time) + Safety Stock
Example: (5 units × 14 days) + 20 = 90 units
Reorder when inventory hits 90 units
```

### Inventory Management Tools

**Spreadsheet** (Free):
- Manual tracking
- Good for under 50 SKUs
- Template: Product | Quantity | Reorder Point | Supplier

**Shopify Built-in**:
- Basic tracking
- Low stock alerts
- Good for under 200 SKUs

**Inventory Management Apps**:
- **Stocky** (Shopify): $99/month, forecasting
- **Cin7**: $299/month, multi-channel
- **TradeGecko**: $299/month, B2B features

> **Vocabulary**:
> - **turnover** /ˈtɜːnəʊvə/ - 周转率
> - **SKU** (Stock Keeping Unit) - 库存单位

## Quality Control

### Inspection Checklist

**Before Ordering**:
- [ ] Order samples from multiple suppliers
- [ ] Test product functionality
- [ ] Check materials and construction
- [ ] Verify packaging quality
- [ ] Compare to competitor products

**First Bulk Order**:
- [ ] Inspect 100% of units (if small order)
- [ ] Random sampling (if large order)
- [ ] Document defects with photos
- [ ] Test shipping packaging
- [ ] Verify product matches sample

**Ongoing Orders**:
- [ ] Random quality checks
- [ ] Track customer complaints
- [ ] Monitor return rates
- [ ] Supplier performance reviews

> **Vocabulary**:
> - **inspection** /ɪnˈspekʃn/ - 检查
> - **sampling** /ˈsɑːmplɪŋ/ - 抽样

### Handling Defects

**With Supplier**:
1. Document issue with photos/videos
2. Contact supplier immediately
3. Request replacement or refund
4. Negotiate compensation for customer impact

**With Customer**:
1. Apologize sincerely
2. Offer immediate solution (refund/replacement)
3. Expedite shipping for replacement
4. Consider goodwill gesture (discount on next order)

## Shipping and Fulfillment

### Fulfillment Options

**Self-Fulfillment**:
- Pack and ship yourself
- Pros: Full control, personal touch
- Cons: Time-consuming, hard to scale
- Best for: Under 20 orders/day

**Third-Party Logistics (3PL)**:
- Warehouse stores and ships for you
- Pros: Scalable, professional
- Cons: Costs ($3-8 per order), less control
- Examples: ShipBob, Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA)

**Hybrid Model**:
- Self-fulfill during testing
- Switch to 3PL when scaling
- Keep some products in-house for customization

> **Vocabulary**:
> - **expedite** /ˈekspədaɪt/ - 加快
> - **fulfillment** /fʊlˈfɪlmənt/ - 履行，配送

### Packaging Strategy

**Functional Packaging**:
- Protects product
- Poly mailers for soft goods
- Boxes for fragile items
- Bubble wrap/packing peanuts

**Branded Packaging**:
- Custom boxes with logo
- Tissue paper in brand colors
- Stickers or stamps
- Thank you cards

**Unboxing Experience**:
- Layered reveal
- Surprise elements (samples, stickers)
- Social media-worthy
- Encourages sharing

**Cost Considerations**:
```
Basic: $0.50-1.00 per order
Branded: $2.00-5.00 per order
Premium: $5.00-10.00 per order

Balance: Cost vs. brand impact
```

> **Vocabulary**:
> - **unboxing** /ʌnˈbɒksɪŋ/ - 开箱

## Common Sourcing Mistakes

### 1. Not Ordering Samples
**Problem**: Receive poor quality bulk order
**Solution**: Always order samples, even if it delays launch

### 2. Choosing Cheapest Supplier
**Problem**: Quality issues, unreliable shipping
**Solution**: Balance price with quality and reliability

### 3. No Backup Supplier
**Problem**: Stockouts when supplier has issues
**Solution**: Identify 2-3 suppliers for key products

### 4. Ignoring Shipping Times
**Problem**: Customer complaints, refund requests
**Solution**: Set realistic expectations, choose faster shipping

### 5. Poor Communication
**Problem**: Misunderstandings, wrong products
**Solution**: Confirm details in writing, use clear specifications
