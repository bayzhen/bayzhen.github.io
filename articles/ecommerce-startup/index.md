This comprehensive series guides you through the entire journey of creating and running a profitable independent e-commerce business. From initial planning to scaling your operations, you'll learn practical strategies and actionable techniques.

## What You'll Learn

- **Foundation**: Market research, niche selection, and business planning
- **Technical Setup**: Platform selection, store configuration, and payment integration
- **Product Strategy**: Sourcing products, inventory management, and pricing
- **Marketing**: SEO, social media, email campaigns, and paid advertising
- **Operations**: Order fulfillment, customer service, and analytics
- **Growth**: Scaling strategies, automation, and long-term profitability

## Prerequisites

- Basic understanding of online shopping
- Willingness to learn and experiment
- Small initial budget (varies by approach)
- CET-6 English level (complex terms annotated)

## Series Structure

This series contains 10 articles that progressively build your knowledge from zero to launch and beyond.
