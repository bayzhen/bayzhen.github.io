Want to start an online store but don't know where to begin? This series walks you through everything - from finding your niche to scaling to six figures.

No fluff, no get-rich-quick schemes. Just practical advice from someone who's been there.

## What You'll Actually Learn

- **Foundation**: How to find a profitable niche and validate demand before spending money
- **Technical Setup**: Choosing a platform and building a store that converts
- **Product Strategy**: Where to source products and how to price them for profit
- **Marketing**: Getting traffic without burning through your budget
- **Operations**: Fulfilling orders and keeping customers happy
- **Growth**: Scaling sustainably without losing your mind

## Who This Is For

- You want to start an e-commerce business but feel overwhelmed
- You have basic English skills (CET-6 level - complex terms are explained)
- You're willing to learn and experiment
- You have a small budget to start (varies by approach, but $500-5000 is typical)

## What Makes This Different

Most e-commerce guides are either too vague ("just believe in yourself!") or too technical (drowning you in jargon). This series finds the middle ground - practical, actionable advice in plain English.

## The Journey

This series has 10 articles that build on each other. You can read them in order or jump to what you need right now.

Start with Article 1 if you're brand new. Skip to Article 4 if you've already picked your niche and need to choose a platform.
